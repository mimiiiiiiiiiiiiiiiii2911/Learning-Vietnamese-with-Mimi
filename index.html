MEET YOUR VIETNAMESE TUTOR

Meet Mimi ✳

Xin chào! I'm Mimi, a Vietnamese tutor from Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

I'm currently studying Oriental Studies at the University of Social Sciences and Humanities – Vietnam National University, Ho Chi Minh City (USSH – VNU-HCM). I enjoy teaching Vietnamese and helping international learners feel more confident communicating with native speakers. Lessons are adapted to each learner's goals and pace.

View Mimi's LinkedIn profile ↗

What can you learn with me?

  • Everyday Vietnamese: Communicate naturally in daily situations.
  • Pronunciation: Improve tones, pronunciation and speaking fluency.
  • Practical conversations: Practice real-life situations, from ordering food to chatting with Vietnamese friends.
  • Vocabulary and grammar: Build a strong foundation with useful words and sentence structures.
  • Vietnamese culture: Discover local expressions, customs, food and everyday life.

My teaching style

Lessons are enjoyable, practical and interactive, with personalized Canva materials, real-life examples, quizzes, speaking activities, homework and feedback. I can teach in English and explain Vietnamese simply. I'm patient and supportive, and mistakes are welcome. As a native speaker from Southern Vietnam, I can help you get familiar with the Southern accent and everyday expressions.

Mistakes are part of learning. Whether you want to travel, connect with family, work in Vietnam or explore a new language, let's make learning Vietnamese fun and meaningful!
